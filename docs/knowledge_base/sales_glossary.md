# Sales Glossary

## Terms & Definitions

**Deal**: A potential or actual transaction with a customer account.

**Stage**: The current position of a deal in the sales process:
- Prospecting: Initial contact
- Qualification: Customer fit confirmed
- Proposal: Formal offer presented
- Negotiation: Contract discussions
- Closed Won: Deal completed successfully
- Closed Lost: Deal unsuccessful

**Quota**: Target revenue amount assigned to a sales rep for a specific period.

**Pipeline**: Sum of all open deal amounts, weighted by win probability.

**Rep (Sales Representative)**: An individual sales person responsible for closing deals.

**Win Probability**: Estimated chance a deal will close, usually 0-100%.

**Close Probability**: Rep's subjective estimate of deal closure likelihood.

**Activity**: Any recorded customer interaction (call, email, meeting, etc.).

**Sales Cycle**: Number of days from deal creation to closure.
