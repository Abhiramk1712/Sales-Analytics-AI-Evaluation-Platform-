# Reporting Templates

## Available Reports

### Executive Weekly
**Audience**: C-level executives, VPs

**Contents**:
- High-level KPIs (total revenue, quota attainment, win rate)
- Key changes and trends
- Risks (underperformers, at-risk deals)
- Recommended actions

**Frequency**: Weekly

### Manager Monthly
**Audience**: Sales managers, team leads

**Contents**:
- Team-level metrics
- Top and at-risk rep identification
- Pipeline health by stage
- Coaching recommendations

**Frequency**: Monthly

### Rep Performance
**Audience**: Individual reps, managers

**Contents**:
- Personal revenue and quota progress
- Win/loss analysis
- Pipeline status
- Activity metrics
- Risk level assessment

**Frequency**: Monthly or on-demand

## Customization
All reports are generated from live data and can be customized by:
- Time period (week, month, quarter)
- Region or team filter
- Metrics included
- Audience focus
