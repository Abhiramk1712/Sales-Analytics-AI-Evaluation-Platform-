# Pipeline Inspection Guide

## Pipeline Health Assessment Framework

A well-functioning sales pipeline requires three things:
1. **Sufficient volume** (coverage ratio)
2. **Healthy stage distribution** (deal progression)
3. **Active engagement** (activity coverage)

## Coverage Ratio Analysis

| Coverage Level | Raw Pipeline | Weighted Pipeline | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | < 2× quota | < 1.5× quota | Emergency pipeline generation sprint |
| At Risk | 2×–3× quota | 1.5×–2× quota | Increase top-of-funnel activity 30% |
| Watch | 3×–4× quota | 2×–3× quota | Monitor weekly, accelerate late-stage deals |
| Healthy | 4×–5× quota | 3×–4× quota | Normal cadence |
| Overpipe | > 5× quota | > 4× quota | Improve deal qualification / selectivity |

## Stage Distribution
Healthy pipeline distribution by deal count:
- Prospecting: 30–40%
- Qualification: 20–25%
- Proposal: 15–20%
- Negotiation: 10–15%
- Balanced between early and late stage

Red flags:
- > 60% of deals in Prospecting (poor qualification)
- < 5% in Negotiation/Proposal (no late-stage momentum)
- All deals in similar size bracket (not enough variety)

## Deal Slip Indicators
A deal is at slip risk when:
1. Expected close date has passed (overdue by any amount)
2. No activity in the last 14 calendar days
3. Still in Prospecting/Qualification stage within 14 days of expected close
4. Rep close probability < 40% within 7 days of expected close
5. Deal age > 2× typical sales cycle for the segment

## Activity Coverage Standards
- Open deals should have at least 1 activity
- Deals in Proposal/Negotiation should have activity within 7 days
- Deals with zero activities in Prospecting/Qualification are at stall risk
- Benchmark: ≥ 3 activities per deal per month in active pipeline

## Weekly Pipeline Review Cadence
1. Check coverage ratio vs quota
2. Identify all overdue deals (expected close < today)
3. Review deals with no activity in 14+ days
4. Confirm deal stage accuracy with reps
5. Update weighted forecast based on stage changes
6. Identify deals to accelerate or disqualify
